About Topone Mechanical Manufacturing

As a leader in the manufacturing industry, Topone Mechanical Manufacturing has a wealth of knowledge and expertise in the metal casting industry. We utilize this experience to provide the best quality and innovative products to the global market.

Our monthly production capacity is exceeding 400 tons with truthful reliability. There are 4 types of typical materials we can produce: Carbon Alloy, Heat-Resistant Steel, Stainless Steel, and Aluminum Alloy.

For more than 20 years we have been the main supplier of SILAR R.S.L. in Italy and KUHN S.A. in France. Our quality is reliable and the further cooperation is promising.
